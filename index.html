The Challenge

Problem Statement

Hospitals face a dual and intensifying challenge: delivering high-quality patient care while drastically reducing their environmental footprint. Healthcare systems are responsible for a significant share of global greenhouse gas emissions, energy consumption, water use, and medical waste. At the same time, they must manage unpredictable patient demand, staff shortages, rising costs, and strict data privacy regulations.

Current Limitations

Current hospital management systems and AI solutions typically address these issues in isolation. Predictive models focus mainly on clinical outcomes without considering environmental impact. Sustainability initiatives often rely on static carbon accounting and fail to integrate real-time clinical and operational data. Existing optimization tools rarely balance clinical quality, cost, staff well-being, and carbon emissions simultaneously, and almost never do so under privacy-preserving constraints.

The Gap

Hospital decision-makers lack a unified, data-driven tool that can:

  • Accurately forecast patient demand and clinical risks
  • Quantify the true environmental cost of clinical and operational decisions
  • Optimize resource allocation across multiple competing objectives
  • Protect sensitive patient and staff data while supporting strategic sustainability goals

Our Solution

GreenCare Twin addresses this gap by developing a multi-objective decision-support system that combines clinical predictive analytics, life-cycle assessment (via openLCA), operations research optimization, and secure data management to enable hospitals to deliver resilient, high-quality, and environmentally responsible care.
